## Pricing Proposal for MyOffer Platform

## 1. Proposal Summary

This proposal covers the delivery of the MyOffer platform scope identified in the workbook-backed requirement baseline, including customer, shopkeeper, homepreneur, sales, admin, and analytics workflows.

The commercial structure is split into two parts:

- **Development charge:** ₹1,20,000 to ₹1,40,000 
- **Monthly maintenance / AMC:** ₹27,000 to ₹30,000

The development timeline is **45 days** from project start, subject to timely approvals, content inputs, and feedback from the client.

---

## 2. Scope Of Work

The development package includes implementation of the current product scope described in the requirement documentation, organized into the following workstreams.

### Customer Module

- Login and logout with phone number or email
- Onboarding with OTP and location capture
- Profile creation with name, address, and preference details
- Shop discovery, search, filters, and offer browsing
- Offer claim flow with QR generation and redemption tracking
- Order history and wallet/reward visibility
- Notifications inbox and basic engagement features

### Shopkeeper Module

- Login and role-based access
- Self onboarding and shop verification flow
- Subscription plan selection and activation
- Shop profile setup and offer management
- Campaign creation, banner support, and redemption handling
- Pricing preview and performance dashboard
- Coupon issue and unique QR/code support

### Homepreneur Module

- First-class persona flow for onboarding
- KYC/business profile capture
- Subscription and offer setup
- QR-based payment receiving workflow
- Campaign and dashboard support
- Rewards and referral-related flows where applicable

### Sales Module

- Sales login and profile setup
- Shopkeeper / homepreneur onboarding support
- Subscription guidance and lead tracking
- Pincode-wise analytics and attribution support

### Admin And Analytics

- Shop approval and governance flows
- Banner and campaign approval workflow
- Pricing and subscription control support
- Coupon monitoring and payment visibility
- Analytics dashboard for customers, shops, homepreneurs, and sales performance
- Fraud and notification controls at a baseline level

## 4. Commercial Offer

### Development Charge

**Quoted range:** ₹1,25,000 to ₹1,30,000 

This covers:

- Requirement finalization and implementation planning
- UI and workflow development for the scoped modules
- Backend integration for the agreed features
- Testing support, bug fixing during delivery, and launch readiness
- One round of final client review adjustments after UAT feedback

### Monthly Maintenance / AMC

**Quoted range:** ₹27,000 to ₹30,000 per month 

This covers:

- Bug fixing after release
- Minor issue resolution and stability support
- Review support for existing flows
- Small corrective changes within the released scope
- Basic coordination for production issues

Maintenance does **not** include new feature development, major UI redesign, third-party integrations outside the agreed scope, or complete workflow expansion.

---

## 5. Payment Terms

- **Development:** 40% advance, 30% at the midpoint review, and 30% before final handover
- **Maintenance:** billed monthly in advance
- Any scope expansion beyond the agreed requirement baseline will be charged separately

---

## 6. Assumptions And Exclusions

### Assumptions

- Client provides timely feedback, content, and approvals
- Scope remains aligned to the current workbook-backed requirement baseline
- All third-party credentials and access required for implementation are provided on time

### Exclusions

- Large post-launch enhancements
- New modules not present in the current requirement baseline
- Paid third-party service subscriptions or platform fees, unless explicitly agreed
- Dedicated 24/7 support unless separately contracted

---

## 7. Support And Review Terms

- Maintenance support covers defect fixes and review-related corrections
- Priority response is limited to business hours unless otherwise agreed
- Any change request that affects scope, timeline, or architecture will be re-estimated
- Revisions are limited to reasonable iterations within the agreed delivery scope

---

## 8. Commercial Positioning

This proposal is designed for a structured 45-day build and launch cycle followed by a predictable monthly support arrangement. It keeps the initial development cost separate from ongoing maintenance so the client has clear visibility on both delivery and post-launch ownership.
